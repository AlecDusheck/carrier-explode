carrier-explode stands on the shoulders of giants. Everything here was built on other people's work.

**Contributors:** [Alec Dusheck](https://dusheck.com).

## Firmware and bundles

- **Apple.** The carrier bundles, modem firmware and settings decoded on this site ship inside Apple's own IPSW restore images and over-the-air (OTA) assets, which Apple hosts on its own download servers. Apple, iOS, iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch are trademarks of Apple Inc.
- **[ipsw.me](https://ipsw.me).** The release catalogue and board configurations come from its public service, which is how each IPSW is found and where Apple hosts it.
- **[AppleDB](https://appledb.dev).** Betas and builds that ipsw.me does not list, and which phone each board in a bundle's file names is, come from AppleDB.
- **Google.** Pixel factory and OTA images are Google's, listed on [Google's Android OTA images page](https://developers.google.com/android/ota). Android field names come from the CarrierConfigManager javadoc in the [Android Open Source Project](https://source.android.com). Pixel, Android and Google are trademarks of Google LLC.

## Prior art

These projects worked out how carrier bundles are extracted and what is in them before this site did.

- [dwilliamsuk/ios-carrier-bundles](https://github.com/dwilliamsuk/ios-carrier-bundles): the IPSW extraction steps.
- [mast3rz3ro/imobilecfbm](https://github.com/mast3rz3ro/imobilecfbm), [mrlnc/ipcc-downloader](https://github.com/mrlnc/ipcc-downloader) and [samsam123.name.my/ipcc](https://samsam123.name.my/ipcc/): downloaders over the same manifest.
- [The Apple Wiki: Carrier Bundle](https://theapplewiki.com/wiki/Carrier_Bundle).
- [JohnBel/EfsTools](https://github.com/JohnBel/EfsTools) and [fenrir-naru/mbn_utils](https://github.com/fenrir-naru/mbn_utils) for Qualcomm EFS and NV names, and 3GPP TS 23.041 and 3GPP2 C.S0016 for the spec-defined formats.

## Images

- **Phone drawings.** The iPhone and Pixel outlines are drawn for this site from shapes measured on Apple's images ([Apple's iPhone comparison](https://www.apple.com/iphone/compare/) and [Apple's iPhone identification guide](https://support.apple.com/108044)) and Google's own images. They are not Apple's or Google's artwork.
- **Version marks.** The iOS and Android release icons are drawn for this site and coloured after each release's own branding.
- **File-type icons** and the site icon are drawn for this site.
